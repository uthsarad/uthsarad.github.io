The following source files are vendored from that commit:
src/lib/blackHolePhysical/model.ts
src/lib/blackHolePhysical/discParticleGlsl.ts
src/lib/blackHolePhysical/webgl.ts
src/shaders/blackHolePhysicalCore.glsl
src/shaders/blackHolePhysicalFragment.glsl

The model, deterministic disc particle generator and both GLSL files are
unmodified. The WebGL host has local lifecycle, abort, frame-rate and
resolution safeguards, a one-frame presentation method, and no new renderer
dependency. React integration is local and compatible with React 18.

Original physical model: https://github.com/ebruneton/black_hole_shader
Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.08735

public/previews/black-hole.jpg -> public/black-hole/preview.jpg

No runtime CDN fetches. No replacement or approximated physics tables.
The renderer preserves Schwarzschild beam tracing, gravitational lensing,
Doppler shift, black-body disc temperature, and original precomputed tables.
The observer is stationary, with camera distance index 850 and time scale
0.35. Upstream defaults for disc density, opacity and temperature are kept.
As in kam-ui, tone mapping is used without the original full bloom pipeline.
